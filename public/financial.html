Money Basics

  • Understand income (money in) vs expenses (money out) every month.
  • Separate needs (food, rent, fees) from wants (eating out, gadgets).
  • Know the difference between cash, bank balance, and digital wallets.
  • Learn simple terms: budget, interest, credit score, EMI, inflation.
  • Set short-, medium-, and long-term financial goals for yourself.

Budgeting Made Simple

  • Track all income (pocket money, scholarship, part-time work) and expenses.
  • Try rules like 50-30-20: needs 50%, wants 30%, savings 20%.
  • Use a notebook or app to record every small payment for one month.
  • Review your spending weekly and cut “leaks” like unused subscriptions.
  • Plan ahead for irregular costs such as exams, festivals, and trips.

Saving & Emergency Funds

  • Pay yourself first: move a fixed amount to savings before spending.
  • Build an emergency fund for sudden expenses like repairs or medical costs.
  • Use separate accounts or labelled jars for goals (laptop, course, trip).
  • Understand simple vs compound interest and aim to earn interest, not pay it.
  • Start small but stay consistent—habits matter more than amount.

Credit, Loans & Debt

  • Credit lets you use money now and repay later, usually with interest.
  • High-interest debt (credit cards, informal loans) becomes expensive fast.
  • Pay EMIs on time to protect your credit score and avoid penalties.
  • Borrow only for clear needs (education, emergencies), not impulse wants.
  • Read loan terms: interest rate, processing fee, tenure, and total cost.

Basic Investing Ideas

  • Investing means using money to buy assets that can grow over time.
  • Diversify: do not put all savings in one place.
  • Start with safer options and learn before trying high-risk products.
  • Invest for long-term goals like higher studies, business, or retirement.
  • Beware of “guaranteed high return” schemes; double-check before trusting.

Protecting Your Money

  • Keep PINs, passwords, and OTPs secret; never share them on calls or messages.
  • Check SMS and bank app regularly to spot unknown transactions early.
  • Use strong passwords and two-factor authentication for banking apps.
  • Understand basics of insurance for health, life, and big assets.
  • Be cautious with online offers and links asking for card details.
